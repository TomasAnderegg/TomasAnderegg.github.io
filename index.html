01Apr. – June 2026

Improving regional knowledge in small language models

Multilingual reasoning via post-training

Context Small language models show strong cultural and linguistic distribution shifts on multilingual reasoning benchmarks, where base models underperform on regional knowledge and non-English languages.

Action Curated 108k deduplicated MCQs from INCLUDE, M3Exam, MMMLU and Global-MMLU, then fine-tuned Qwen3-1.7B via LoRA SFT followed by GRPO with verifiable rewards.

Result Improved validation Pass@1 from 41.6% to 65.9% (+24.3 pts), with the largest gains on Hindi and Chinese, and contributed the expert to a TIES-merged multi-domain model (Math, General Knowledge, Safety and Multilingual Reasoning).

02Feb. – June 2026

CHAR: deep learning for autonomous vehicles

End-to-end planner for autonomous driving

Context End-to-end trajectory predictors on nuPlan often suffer from a persistent sim-to-real visual gap and causal confusion when relying on raw multi-frame visual inputs.

Action Built a camera + kinematic-history + command fusion model (EfficientNet-B0, bidirectional GRU with temporal attention, auxiliary depth/segmentation heads), then adapted it to real sensor data via mixed sim-to-real training.

Result Reduced validation ADE from 1.76 to 1.42, demonstrating strong sim-to-real generalization despite a data imbalance, achieving an excellent grade.

03Feb. – June 2026

Enhancing SmolVLA with multimodal robustness

Multimodal vision-language-action model

Context VLA policies for robotic manipulation typically rely on a single visual (RGB) encoder and degrade sharply under sensor noise or missing modalities.

Action Replaced SmolVLA's SigLIP encoder with a 4M-21 (any-to-any model) multimodal encoder and trained with a modality-dropout curriculum on the LIBERO benchmark.

Result Traced the policy's transfer failure, through a top-down failure analysis and t-SNE, to a latent-scale mismatch between the multimodal embeddings and the action expert; redesigning the embedding-space training loss to align them.

Navigation system for Thymio robot

Autonomous mobile robotics

Context Reliable navigation in dynamic environments requires combining global path planning with reactive local obstacle avoidance and fusing sensor information.
